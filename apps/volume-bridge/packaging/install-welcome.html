VolumeBridge {{VERSION}}

Use macOS volume keys and the system slider with your LG display.

  • Connect one LG ULTRAGEAR+ by USB-C and keep your Mac signed in and unlocked.
  • Installation adds an audio driver and background service. No app or development tools are required.
  • Audio pauses briefly during installation. On first installation, VolumeBridge becomes the selected output at a low volume.
  • The installer saves your display volume, calibrates it, and verifies the result. Updates preserve your current settings.

Use Uninstall VolumeBridge.pkg to restore the original settings and remove VolumeBridge. Keep the display connected during removal.
